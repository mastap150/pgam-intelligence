01 The property

A travel publisher that owns its own demand stack.

Destination.com is an owned-and-operated property of PGAM Media — an ad tech company that runs its own SSP. That means no reseller between you and the inventory, no opaque supply chain, and a media plan that can be built across editorial, email, and commerce in one conversation.

Editorial promise
Guides written from the ground, not assembled from other people's listicles. No paid rankings, ever — sponsorship buys placement and attention, never a position in a ranking.
Publishing cadence
New guides weekly and existing guides revisited on a rolling schedule, so the catalogue stays current on visas, prices, and seasonality rather than aging into a liability.
Built for intent
The reader arrives mid-decision — comparing two islands, checking a visa rule, pricing a safari. Editorial is organised around that decision, which is why commerce performs here.

Three things make this audience worth more than its size suggests:

01 · High-ticket category
A single reader decision is worth thousands of dollars in flights, hotels, tours, insurance, and cards. Travel intent monetises at a multiple of general interest.
02 · A points & miles pillar
Award travel is the highest-value vertical in this category, and it is a first-class section here rather than a footnote — the audience for it is self-selected and financially engaged.
03 · A closed loop
Site, newsletter, and app share one identity layer, so a campaign can start in editorial and finish in the inbox against the same segment.

02 The website

Four pillars, six regions, one honest voice.

The site is a hub-and-spoke library: region hubs feed country guides, country guides feed city and experience guides, and every guide routes back to the planning and points content that converts. Advertisers buy into that structure — a region, an experience type, or a decision moment — not a run-of-network blur.

Pillar 01

Destinations

Europe, Asia, the Americas, Africa, the Pacific, and the Middle East — region hubs down to city level, with guide counts and freshness dates on every node.

Pillar 02

Experiences

Adventure, food and drink, culture and art, wellness. Readers filter by how they travel, not only where — which is exactly how brands want to target.

Pillar 03

Travel Guides

Visas, best-time-to-visit, insurance, packing, safety, airports, booking windows. The highest-intent content on the site and the last thing read before booking.

Pillar 04

Points & Miles

Card bonuses, transfer sweet spots, hotel redemptions, tracked weekly. Premium CPMs and the strongest card-acquisition audience on the property.

AI Trip Planner
Readers enter dates, budget, party, and trip type and get a day-by-day itinerary with a costed breakdown and live hotel and flight options. Every plan is a declared intent — destination, dates, budget band — and sponsorable as such.
Native app · iOS & Android
Guides, saved trips, and planner access in a signed-in app, giving a persistent identity and a push channel alongside email.
Live commerce rails
Hotels, vacation rentals, packages, cars, tours, and activities all route through live affiliate integrations with Expedia Group, Viator, and GetYourGuide. Booking behaviour is measured, not assumed.
Traffic & audience detail
Full traffic, geography, and viewability reporting is shared on request under NDA, alongside a current rate card and availability calendar.

06 Standards

Clean supply, clean pages, clean disclosure.

Most of what depresses performance in this category is self-inflicted: too many units, unverified supply, and disclosure treated as an afterthought. Here is what we hold to.

Verified, direct supply

Authorised seats are declared DIRECT in ads.txt and machine-checked every day. A missing or downgraded line pages an engineer the same morning.

Owned and operated

One publisher, one domain, no arbitraged or resold traffic in the path. You know exactly what you bought.

Three units, hard cap

No more than three in-content ads per article, none inside the first 200 words, and never two units stacked. Density is capped to protect viewability, not inflate it.

No dark patterns

No pop-ups, no pop-unders, no interstitials, no auto-playing video. Core Web Vitals are a revenue input here, not a compliance chore.

Labelled, always

Sponsored content carries its label above the fold, paid links are marked, and affiliate relationships are disclosed to FTC standard on every page that carries them.

Editorial independence

Sponsorship never buys a ranking, a rewrite, or the removal of a recommendation. It is the reason the audience trusts a click, and the reason a click is worth buying.
