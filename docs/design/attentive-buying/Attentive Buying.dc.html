Measurement methodology
Attention is measured, not modelled.
Every score on this platform comes from observed behaviour on delivered impressions. We do not infer attention from publisher reputation, from price, or from whether an impression was technically viewable. If we cannot measure a placement, it has no score — and we say so rather than estimating one.
The four inputs
{{ m.name }} {{ m.weight }} of the score
{{ m.body }}
{{ m.value }}
this deal
The four inputs are combined and normalised to a 0–100 scale against a rolling 30-day distribution of all CTV placements we measure. A score of 50 is the median placement, not a pass mark. Because the distribution moves, a score is only comparable to other scores from the same window — which is why packages are rebuilt nightly rather than scored once.
What attention is not
✕
{{ n.title }} {{ n.body }}
Coverage
{{ c.label }} {{ c.value }}
Where coverage is partial, the deal's score is calculated on the measured portion only and the coverage figure travels with it.
Independence
Measurement runs on the impression stream, not on our own sales data. Nobody at PGAM can adjust a placement's score, and a publisher cannot pay to raise one. If a placement's score falls, it leaves the curated pool that night.
Independent verification is on the roadmap for v2. Until it lands, treat these as our own measurements and hold us to them.
