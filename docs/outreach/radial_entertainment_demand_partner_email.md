**Subject:** About PGAM Media — demand partnership with Radial Entertainment

Hi [First name],

Thanks for the note, and for the interest in a demand partnership. Below is a fuller picture of PGAM you're welcome to circulate internally.

**Who we are**

PGAM Media is a programmatic infrastructure company — not an agency. We built a unified SSP and DSP because we got tired of watching media budgets evaporate inside opaque auction chains. We're headquartered in Coral Gables, Florida, and we transact across CTV, OTT, online video, display, and mobile. **How we buy**

Our DSP powers both managed service and self-serve buying across two lines: brand awareness on premium CTV/OLV, and performance campaigns priced on cost-per-qualifying-call for call-driven advertisers. Every campaign ships with per-impression attention scoring and full log-level reporting — the publisher, the placement, the attention score, and the price — as standard, not as an upsell.

**What we bring to Radial**

1. **Demand that matches your audience.** A ~70,000-title catalog across FilmRise and Shout! Studios — classics, true crime, cult film, non-scripted — indexes exactly where our highest-paying direct-response and brand verticals want to be. That's an audience match, not a remnant-fill relationship.
2. **Attention-based pricing.** PGAM Attention scores each impression on engagement, completion, and viewability. Inventory that holds attention earns a premium in our auction, and we activate it through Deal IDs so your best avails aren't priced like the open market.
3. **Proof of outcome.** Unified attribution across CTV and digital — view-through, pixel-based conversions, cross-device via Kochava, and TFN-matched call attribution down to creative, DMA, and daypart. Advertisers who can see outcomes renew and increase budgets on the inventory that produced them.
4. **Supply-path hygiene.** We run automated, continuous audits on sellers.json, ads.txt, and schain across every partner we transact with, plus demand-quality filtering and real-time floor optimization. Our integration will be direct and declared correctly.
5. **Operating discipline.** Creative QA, pacing, and brand-safety lists handled by our AdOps team; weekly written optimization recaps and quarterly reviews. Underperforming creatives and sources get paused inside 72 hours, not at month-end.

**Getting started**

Integration is straightforward — oRTB endpoint, VAST tags, or PMP/programmatic guaranteed deal IDs, whichever matches your current trafficking. Typical path: terms and specs exchanged in the first few days, tags built and QA'd, a soft-launch test flight to validate delivery and attribution, then scale. We'd want to start with a defined test so both sides are looking at real numbers before committing volume.
