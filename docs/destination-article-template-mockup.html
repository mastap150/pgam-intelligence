Breeze Airways Pauses Four Florida Routes Through Early October

The low-cost carrier is suspending service on four Florida routes until October 6, affecting travelers booked out of Tampa, Jacksonville and Fort Myers.

What travelers need to know

What happened
Breeze is pausing four Florida routes from August 25 through October 6.
Who's affected
Anyone booked on TPA–HPN, JAX–PVD, RSW–BDL or TPA–ORF in that window.
Where
Tampa, Jacksonville and Fort Myers.
When
Aug 25 – Oct 6, 2026. Service resumes Oct 7.
Why it matters
These are the only nonstops on three of the four pairs — rebooking means a connection.
What to do
Breeze is offering a full refund or free rebooking. Request it in My Trips; it is not automatic.

Breeze Airways will suspend service on four Florida routes for roughly six weeks this autumn, the carrier confirmed in a schedule filing published Wednesday. The pause runs from August 25 to October 6, with service resuming the following day.

The affected routes all connect Florida leisure markets with the Northeast — a pairing that reliably softens between the end of summer travel and the start of the winter season.

What it means if you're booked

Breeze's contract of carriage entitles you to a full refund when the airline cancels, regardless of fare type — including Nice fares, which are normally non-refundable. That right is not exercised automatically: you have to request it.

Affected guests will be contacted directly and offered a full refund or rebooking at no additional cost.Breeze Airways, schedule filing, Aug 20

Why Breeze keeps trimming Florida

This is the third seasonal pause Breeze has taken on Florida–Northeast flying in eighteen months. The pattern is consistent: the carrier adds capacity into the winter, holds it through spring break, then pulls back sharply once school resumes.

For travelers, the practical read is that Breeze's Florida network is best treated as seasonal. If you're planning autumn travel, check our Florida travel guide and best time to visit Florida.
